The game

First-person conservation action in a Kruger-inspired reserve. Explore the park, confront poachers, restrain them, and escort them to arrest zones across tutorial and timed patrol modes.

My role

Programmer and game developer on a four-person team with Xander Jacobs, Angus van Eeden, and Liam Fullard. Flagship project — deepest production experience across controller, combat, UI, and progression.

Systems

Built in Unreal Engine 5 with Blueprints and UMG. Focused on player feel, combat readability, main-menu flow, and roguelike / progression loops that structure each patrol.

Highlights

  • Player controller for exploration, restraint, and escort
  • Combat systems shared with the wider gameplay loop
  • Main menu and UMG player-facing flow
  • Roguelike / progression scaffolding for patrol runs
