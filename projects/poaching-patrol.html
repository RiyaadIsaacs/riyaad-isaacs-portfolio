Poaching Patrol

First-person conservation action in a Kruger-inspired reserve — explore, confront poachers, restrain and escort them to arrest zones.

Synopsis

Step into the boots of a ranger in Poaching Patrol. Explore a Kruger-inspired reserve, respond to threats against wildlife, and bring poachers into custody. Stopping a poacher is only part of the job — restrain them, keep the escort secure, and lead them to an arrest zone. Manage supplies and use the patrol map to navigate the reserve, from a guided First Day Tutorial into timed Normal Patrol objectives.

Project brief

Produce a playable Unreal Engine 5 first-person action-adventure that demonstrates advanced engine workflow — Blueprints, environment assembly, player HUD / minimap, restraint and escort systems, and a Windows shipping package for public distribution on itch.io.

My role & contribution

Programmer and game developer (credited as Riyaad Isaacs). Poaching Patrol is my most recent project and the one I have the most experience on. I built the player controller, part of the combat system, the main menu, and the roguelike / progression systems.

Technologies
