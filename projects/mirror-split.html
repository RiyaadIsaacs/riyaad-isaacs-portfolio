Mirror Split

Bullet heaven where one character is fought in two places at once — a split-screen mirrored arena that turns attention into the core skill.

Synopsis

Mirror Split follows an artifact hunter caught in a reality-splitting trap while monsters close in. Players fight to survive as long as possible, leveling up by clearing enemies across a split-screen world where one half is inverted — forcing dual attention as the hunt intensifies.

Project brief

Prototype a Windows auto-shooter / bullet heaven for The Campus Game Jam 2026 that differentiates through dual-world split-screen control, inverted inputs on one plane, and a readable survival loop of combat, XP, and escalation.
