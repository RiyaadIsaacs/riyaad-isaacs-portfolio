Mirror Split

Bullet heaven where one character is fought in two places at once — a split-screen mirrored arena that turns attention into the core skill. Winner of Most Mechanically Elegant at The Campus Game Jam 2026, with prize money.

Synopsis

Mirror Split follows an artifact hunter caught in a reality-splitting trap while monsters close in. Players fight to survive as long as possible, leveling up by clearing enemies across a split-screen world where one half is inverted — forcing dual attention as the hunt intensifies.

Project brief

Prototype a Windows auto-shooter / bullet heaven for The Campus Game Jam 2026 that differentiates through dual-world split-screen control, inverted inputs on one plane, and a readable survival loop of combat, XP, and escalation.

Award

Mirror Split won Most Mechanically Elegant at The Campus Game Jam 2026, recognised for the dual-world control idea and how cleanly the survival loop holds it together. The award included prize money.

My role & contribution

Programmer and game developer (itch: Mettlefox). I built the player controller, and worked on the game manager and enemy spawning systems that keep the dual-world survival loop running.
