Mirror Split

Bullet heaven where one character is fought in two places at once — a split-screen mirrored arena that turns attention into the core skill.

Synopsis

Mirror Split is a bullet heaven in which players control the protagonist in the normal world and a mirror dimension simultaneously. The top and bottom halves show the same arena, but the bottom is flipped and enemy spawns differ. Surviving means reading both spaces while waves escalate and power fantasy meets tension.

Project brief

Develop a PC-focused bullet heaven that differentiates through dual-character / dual-screen control. Expand from a playable core loop (movement, combat, XP, level-ups, skills and passives) toward a commercially packaged experience under the RETROBYTE studio brand — including content expansion, balancing, presentation, and release readiness.
