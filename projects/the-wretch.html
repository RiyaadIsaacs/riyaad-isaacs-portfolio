The Wretch

A short one-level dungeon demo: dive into chambers tied to a beast in the earth’s core, and push far enough to stop its dungeons from spreading.

Synopsis

The Wretch is a short one-level demo about diving into dungeons connected to a mysterious beast living deep in the earth’s core, feeding off adventurers that dare enter the chambers it creates. The goal is to conquer those dungeons and ultimately stop them from growing into the world.

Project brief

Ship a playable Windows dungeon-crawler prototype with readable exploration, threat, and HUD feedback — packaged for public download on itch.io.

My role & contribution

Programmer and game developer (itch: Mettlefox). Built and packaged the playable demo systems that drive exploration, UI, and the dungeon run loop for this short prototype.
