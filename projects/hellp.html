HELLp

Action-adventure roguelike — descend into the abyss to purify lost souls, armed with a divine halo that is both weapon and light.

Synopsis

HELLp is an action-adventure roguelike RPG where you traverse deep into the abyss of hell to save and purify lost souls and earn your place in heaven. Your divine halo — gifted by God — doubles as weapon and light source, shifting from a holy glow to crimson red the deeper you battle for redemption.

Project brief

Ship a short, playable Windows prototype for Ludum Dare 57 that demonstrates combat feel, atmospheric descent, and technical packaging for public download on itch.io.
